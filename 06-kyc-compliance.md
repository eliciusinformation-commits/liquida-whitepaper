# 6. Student-Exclusive KYC & Compliance Framework

To maintain regulatory integrity and prevent illicit actors from exploiting the protocol, Liquida Exchange enforces a strict, privacy-preserving **Student-Exclusive KYC** mechanism.

## 6.1 Verification Protocol
Access to $LPUSD minting, high-yield vaults, and governance participation requires cryptographic proof of active student enrollment. Users authenticate using official institutional credentials:
* Valid University Registration Form / Certificate of Registration (COR).
* Verified Institutional Student Email (`.edu.ph` or recognized university domains).
* Government-issued digital identity integration via eGovPH national ID verification.

Zero-knowledge proofs (ZKP) are integrated to verify student status without exposing sensitive personal identification data to on-chain observers, ensuring absolute data privacy while maintaining strict regulatory compliance.
