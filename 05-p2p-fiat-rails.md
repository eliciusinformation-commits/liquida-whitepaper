# 5. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Local Fiat Rails (GCash, Maya, MariBank, GoTyme)

Bridging decentralized protocols with everyday student life requires seamless integration with existing domestic financial plumbing in the Philippines. Liquida Exchange bypasses sluggish traditional wire transfers by implementing an escrow-secured Peer-to-Peer (P2P) fiat gateway network supporting the nation's leading electronic money issuers (EMIs) and digital banks.

## 5.1 Supported Financial Networks
* **GCash & Maya:** Utilized for high-frequency, micro-denomination conversions (PHP 50 to PHP 5,000) leveraging QRPh standards and mobile number transfers.
* **MariBank & GoTyme:** Employed for larger term-savings deposits, institutional yield routing, and frictionless digital banking settlements with zero transfer friction.

## 5.2 Secure P2P Escrow Mechanics
When a student initiates a fiat-to-$LPUSD purchase, the platform locks the corresponding digital tokens in a smart contract escrow. The buyer transfers local fiat directly to the verified P2P facilitator's GCash, Maya, MariBank, or GoTyme account. Upon fiat receipt confirmation, the smart contract releases $LPUSD instantly to the student's non-custodial wallet, ensuring zero counterparty default risk.

* **Dispute Resolution:** Automated timeout mechanisms and multi-signature moderator overrides managed by the DAO Treasury handle edge cases, protecting both student buyers and liquidity providers.
