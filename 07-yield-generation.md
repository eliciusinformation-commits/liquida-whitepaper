# 7. Yield Generation Engine & Revenue Model

Liquida Exchange generates robust protocol revenue by capturing micro-transaction volume while deploying treasury reserves into secure, high-yield decentralized financial instruments.

## 7.1 Revenue Streams
* **Interest Margin Spread:** The difference between borrowing interest rates and depositor rewards is retained by the DAO Treasury.
* **P2P Gateway Conversion Fees:** A minimal 0.3% to 0.5% settlement fee applied during fiat-to-crypto and crypto-to-fiat swaps.
* **Liquidation Penalties:** Fees collected from clearing under-collateralized positions, 50% of which funds automatic $LBASE token buybacks.

## 7.2 Achievable Student Yields (8.5% - 12.4% APY)
Protocol vaults deploy student-held $LPUSD into over-collateralized money markets, delta-neutral staking strategies, and blue-chip real-world asset (RWA) token pools. This enables the protocol to distribute consistent yields ranging from **8.5% to 12.4% APY** directly to student depositors—outperforming traditional commercial bank savings rates by over 40x.
