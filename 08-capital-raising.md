# 8. Capital Raising Structure & Equity Share Allocation

To accelerate development, fund rigorous third-party smart contract audits, and scale campus onboarding operations under the direct leadership of solo developer Rogelio Patricio Jr., Liquida Exchange is raising initial operational capital through structured **equity shares** in the foundational technology entity.

## 8.1 Equity Offering Structure
* **Total Seed Raise Target:** PHP 10,000,000 ($175,000 USD equivalent) structured as convertible equity shares.
* **Investor Perks:** Pro-rata warrants for future $LBASE token allocations, ensuring aligned incentives between equity holders and protocol growth.
* **Capital Deployment Breakdown:**
  * Smart Contract Audits & Security (40%)
  * Legal Structuring & Regulatory Compliance (20%)
  * Campus Ambassador Programs & Marketing (25%)
  * Infrastructure, RPC Nodes & Operational Reserves (15%)
